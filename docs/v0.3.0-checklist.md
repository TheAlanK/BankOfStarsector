# v0.3.0-beta — "Collateral & Credit"

More ways to borrow, real consequences for each, and better tools to see what you owe.

Branch: `release/v0.3.0-beta` (from `main` at v0.2.1-beta). Effort: S = a day or two, M = a few days, L = a week or more.

**Order:** 0 → 1 → 2 → 5 → 6 → 3 → 4 → 7. The cheap items that finish the credit score come first, and the big gameplay features follow.

## 1. Credit-builder loan (S) ✅
A small loan whose money stays locked at the bank until it is repaid. It's the realistic way to start a credit file.

**Design**
- [x] New `LoanType.BUILDER`: 10k–50k, 12 months (configurable), fixed 1%/month, no minimum score. Offered while the player has no score or a score under 500 (`builderMaxScore`), one at a time.
- [x] The loan amount is held on the loan itself (`BankAccount.heldFunds`, a new saved field that loads as 0 from old saves) instead of being paid to the player.
- [x] Paid off: the held amount is released to the player.
- [x] Default: the held amount pays the loan first; any excess goes back to the player.
- [x] Reports to the credit bureau as `PERSONAL` (an installment account). A default is reported as a charge-off, not a repossession.
- [x] Doesn't count toward the loan limit, gets no Collection Fleet (the bank holds the money), and stays available during the bankruptcy lockout (it exists to rebuild credit).

**Implementation**
- [x] `LoanType` (`isBuilder`, `getTermMonths`), `LoanManager.takeLoan`, payoff release and `applyHeldFunds` at default and in `BankruptcyManager.fileBankruptcy`.
- [x] `LoanManager.whyNot(type, data)`: one place for loan eligibility, used by the terminal (it was duplicated in two places).
- [x] `CollectionManager`: no fleet for builder loans. `InterestEngine`: fixed builder rate.
- [x] Terminal Loans tab: builder offered first (hidden once the score is good; the only offer during the bankruptcy lockout), "held at the bank" line on the loan. The branch dialog opens the terminal, so it needed no change.
- [x] Settings `builderMinAmount`, `builderMaxAmount`, `builderTermMonths`, `builderRate`, `builderMaxScore` in `bos_settings.json` and `BankSettings`; max, term and rate in `LunaSettings.csv`.
- [x] Net worth counts the held money as the player's (terminal Overview and NexusUI), with a "Held at the bank" line in the Overview.
- [x] NexusUI: "Held at Bank" row, held amount on the loan row, `heldFunds` in the JSON.
- [x] Strings: en and pt-BR.
- [x] Found while testing: defaults reached the credit report only if the loan was still in default at month end. `CreditBureau.recordChargeOff` now records them when they happen, and a charged-off loan stops adding late marks every month.

**Tests (BankSim)**
- [x] A thin file with a builder loan becomes scoreable at 6 months.
- [x] Paying off releases the held funds exactly once.
- [x] Default: the deposit pays the debt, no Collection Fleet, reported as a charge-off.
- [x] Not offered with a good score, nor while another one is open; offered during the bankruptcy lockout while other loans are not.
- [x] Save baseline updated: `LoanType.BUILDER`, `BankAccount.heldFunds`.

## 2. Revolving credit line (M) ✅
A Confederation credit line with a limit you draw and repay freely, billed monthly with a minimum payment.

**Design decisions**
- [x] **Data model:** a `BankAccount` of the new `LoanType.CREDIT_LINE` (`isRevolving()`), so loans, collection, seizure, bankruptcy, the credit bureau and NexusUI keep working. New saved fields on `BankAccount`: `creditLimit`, `statementBalance`, `paidSinceStatement`, `lastInterest`, `onTimeStreak`, `autopayFull`.
- [x] **Statement cycle:** at month end the balance becomes the statement balance, with a minimum due of interest + 1% of the balance (at least 1k, or the whole balance if smaller), due at the next month end.
- [x] **Grace period:** no interest when the previous statement was paid in full; otherwise interest on the whole balance.
- [x] **Limit:** 50k / 200k / 500k by bracket (fair / good / excellent; score 500+). +20% every 6 on-time statements, up to 2× the bracket's limit. A late payment resets the streak and cuts the limit by 25% (never below the balance). Default or bankruptcy closes it to new draws (limit 0); paid down to zero, it closes.
- [x] "Sovereign Credit Line" renamed "Sovereign Loan" in the strings (id unchanged).
- [x] One line at a time; it doesn't count toward the loan limit; opening it is a hard inquiry.

**Implementation**
- [x] Terminal Loans tab: a credit-line panel (offer, or balance/limit/available/utilization, statement, minimum, grace notice, interest charged, late amount, autopay mode) with draw (10/25/50/100% of available), pay minimum, pay statement, pay balance, autopay mode and close buttons. Overview: credit-line row. The line is not listed among installment loans.
- [x] Month end in `BankEconomyListener`: the statement is billed with the installments; autopay pays the full statement or the minimum (per line; new lines default to full statement); `LoanManager.reviewCreditLines` raises or cuts limits after missed payments are marked.
- [x] Missed minimum → past due → the existing 30/60/90 reporting and collection phases.
- [x] **Credit bureau**
  - [x] New tradeline category `REVOLVING`; tradelines report the credit limit.
  - [x] Lenders report the **statement balance** (paying in full afterwards doesn't hide it; paying down before the statement does).
  - [x] "Amounts owed" uses utilization (≤ 10% best, 0% slightly lower, > 30% hurts, > 90% hurts badly), weighted 65% against the installment balance ratio when both exist. New reason code "credit line balance too high compared to its limit".
  - [x] Credit mix: the top of the scale needs both installment and revolving credit.
  - [x] Utilization has no memory: paying it down raises the score at the next report.
- [x] Monthly report: the minimum or statement paid by autopay is booked with the installments.
- [x] NexusUI: "Credit Line" card (balance, limit, available, utilization, minimum due, statement left, autopay mode) with a "Pay statement" button; `creditLine` object in the JSON; the line is left out of the loans list.
- [x] Settings for the rate, limits per bracket, minimum payment, increases and cuts in `bos_settings.json`; rate and limits in LunaLib. Strings en and pt-BR, credit tab utilization row and tip.
- [x] Save baseline: +12 entries. Found by the guard: a `-1` "no line" sentinel in the cached credit report would load as `0` from older saves (XStream skips initializers), so the display now checks `hasRevolving()`.

**Tests (BankSim)**
- [x] 10% vs 90% utilization: 687 vs 602; a month after paying 90% down to 10%: 686 (10% throughout: 696).
- [x] Statement paid in full: no interest over 3 months; minimum only: interest from the second statement.
- [x] A missed minimum is reported 30 days late, restricts banking, blocks draws and cuts the limit.
- [x] Limit 200k → 240k after 6 on-time statements.
- [x] Ten draw/repay cycles in one month add exactly one month of history and no inquiries.
- [x] Eligibility: not offered to a thin file; good score → 200k; one line only; not counted toward the loan limit. Closing works only at zero balance and closes the account on the report.

## 3. Colony-secured loans (L) ✅
Lower rates, but a defaulted loan puts the colony in receivership, and with Nexerelin the PBC forecloses and auctions it.

**Spikes**
- [x] **Nexerelin invasion API.** `new InvasionIntel(attacker, source, target, fp, 1).init()` with `InvasionFleetManager.getWantedFleetSize` / `getInvasionSizeMult`, exactly as Nexerelin's own `SpawnInvasionFleet` console command; called directly through `NexerelinBridge` (no reflection). Market hand-over: `SectorManager.transferMarket`.
- [x] **War requirement.** Nexerelin invasions terminate themselves when the attacker is not hostile to the target ("no longer hostile"), so a foreclosure needs the PBC hostile to the player for its duration.

**Decision (user, 2026-10-10):** receivership first, then war (Nexerelin), then the colony is **auctioned to a major faction**, priced on development, structures and natural resources/traits, so **the PBC never grows**. Additional rules chosen: proceeds pay the loan and any surplus goes to the player (shortfall stays owed); bidders are the major factions with territory not hostile to the PBC; second-price auction with a 70% reserve; without Nexerelin it stays at receivership.

**Design**
- [x] Collateral: a player colony of size ≥ 4. Appraisal (`ColonyAppraisal`): development 100k × 2.5^(size−3); structures (build cost, +25% improved, AI cores and special items at base price); resources and traits by tier × 60k; a year of positive net income; × hazard factor (150% − hazard/2, 50–125%). Maximum loan = 60% of the appraisal (capped at 3M). Rate: 25% below the base rate, then the usual credit and war adjustments. Term 24 months, minimum score 450.
- [x] Lien: condition "PBC Lien" on the colony (no effect, visible), removed when the loan closes.
- [x] Abandoned or decivilized colony → the whole balance falls due at once (default, charge-off). Captured by another faction → the loan continues unsecured.
- [x] **Default → receivership:** condition "PBC Receivership" (−3 stability); the colony's whole positive net income goes to the loan at month end (its own report line, excluded from the general garnishment). No Collection Fleet for a loan secured by a colony.
- [x] **Foreclosure (Nexerelin):** still in default 60 days later, the PBC's relation to the player goes to −0.65 (saved and restored later) and an invasion is sent from the PBC's largest market; another every 60 days if it fails.
- [x] **Auction:** when the PBC holds the colony, the next day it is sold: each bidder bids 80–120% of the appraisal × proximity (1.2 same system … 0.8 beyond 40 ly) × relation with the PBC; the winner pays the second-highest bid (at least the 70% reserve); no bidder → independents at the reserve. Nexerelin transfers the market. The price pays the loan (closed as seized: a repossession on the credit report), the surplus goes to the player, relations are restored, the conditions removed.
- [x] Paying the loan current lifts receivership and ends the foreclosure (relations restored).
- [x] Credit bureau: new category `SECURED`.

**Implementation**
- [x] `LoanType.SECURED`, `BankAccount` collateral fields, `LoanManager` (`pledgeableColonies`, `securedMaxFor`, `takeSecuredLoan`, eligibility), `ColonyAppraisal`, `collection/ForeclosureManager` (daily state machine, auction, receivership income), conditions `condition/BOSLienCondition` and `BOSReceivershipCondition` (+ `data/campaign/market_conditions.csv`), `NexerelinBridge`/`NexerelinCompat` (invasion, transfer).
- [x] Terminal: "Colony-Secured Loans" section (each colony with appraisal breakdown and maximum, 25/50/75/100% buttons with confirmation), collateral and receivership/foreclosure lines on the loan. Branch dialog: colony-secured option (colony → slider → terms → sign). Quote shows the collateral and what a default does.
- [x] NexusUI: lien/receivership/foreclosure on the loan row (marked as a problem when in trouble); `collateral` array in the JSON (loan, colony, appraisal, state).
- [x] Settings in `bos_settings.json` (LTV, discount, size, penalties, delays, relation, reserve, appraisal parameters) and LunaLib (LTV, foreclosure delay, stability penalty). Strings en and pt-BR. LORE.md: foreclosure auctions on Bullion.
- [x] Save baseline +9 entries; `docs/SAVE_COMPAT.md` notes the saved condition ids.

**Tests (BankSim, with fake colonies and factions)**
- [x] Appraisal adds up exactly (3.73M for the test colony); a 200% hazard halves it.
- [x] Signing: cheaper than unsecured (2.25% vs 3.5%), capped by the loan-to-value, pays out and puts a lien; one loan per colony; paying off releases the lien.
- [x] Receivership: on default (condition on the colony), no Collection Fleet, the whole income (100k) goes to the loan, no war without Nexerelin, paying the amount due lifts it.
- [x] With Nexerelin (simulated): foreclosure war at −0.65; when the PBC holds the colony it is auctioned to a major faction, the loan is paid, the surplus returned, relations restored, conditions removed; pirates and factions hostile to the PBC don't bid.
- [x] Collateral captured → loan unsecured; abandoned → whole balance due.
- [ ] In game (manual): a real foreclosure with Nexerelin (invasion, capture, auction, market transfer), the colony conditions and the terminal/branch screens.

## 4. Fleet insurance (M)
House Varenne's underwriters (see LORE.md) insure your ships.

**Design**
- [ ] Policy: covers X% (configurable, e.g. 60%) of each insured ship's base value, with a deductible per claim.
- [ ] Premium per month = rate × insured value × risk factor. The risk factor comes from the credit score bracket (real insurers use credit-based insurance scores) and from claims in the last 12 months.
- [ ] Waiting period: no claims in the first 30 days of a policy.
- [ ] Ships lost while the premium is past due are not covered; two missed premiums cancel the policy.
- [ ] Payout cap per month; destroyed ships that are recovered in the post-battle dialog are not paid.

**Implementation**
- [ ] `CampaignEventListener.reportBattleFinished`: compare the fleet before and after the battle. Pay only for ships that are gone at the end of the encounter, not just disabled (they can still be recovered).
- [ ] The premium in the monthly report (FDNode).
- [ ] Terminal: new "Insurance" tab (coverage, premium, claims history). Branch dialog option to buy or cancel the policy.
- [ ] Strings en and pt-BR; settings.

**Tests (BankSim)**
- [ ] The premium changes with fleet value and score bracket.
- [ ] A loss is paid minus the deductible; a recovered ship is not paid.
- [ ] Waiting period, lapse, and the monthly cap.

**Design**
- [ ] Collateral: a player colony of size ≥ 4 (configurable). Maximum loan = monthly colony net income × term × a factor (e.g. 0.6). Rate: the loan type's rate minus 25%.
- [ ] Lien: memory key `$bos_lien` on the market, plus a condition "PBC Lien" with no effect, so it shows on the colony screen.
- [ ] Abandoning or losing the colony (`reportPlayerAbandonedColony`, `reportColonyDecivilized`, or a market owner change) means immediate default.
- [ ] **On default with Nexerelin:** a PBC "Foreclosure" invasion of the colony.
- [ ] **On default without Nexerelin:** receivership. 100% of that colony's net income goes to the bank, and a condition "PBC Receivership" adds a stability penalty. It ends when the debt is paid.
- [ ] Redemption: paying the past-due amount cures the loan before the foreclosure lands. After foreclosure, the colony is lost and the debt is settled.
- [ ] Credit bureau: new category `SECURED`; foreclosure is recorded as a `REPOSSESSION` event.

**Implementation**
- [ ] Loan option: pick a colony in the terminal and in the branch dialog.
- [ ] Hooks:
  - [ ] `CampaignEventListener.reportPlayerAbandonedColony`;

  - [ ] a daily ownership check in `BankCampaignScript`.
- [ ] `AssetSeizureManager` / garnishment: per-market receivership.
- [ ] Intel notices for the lien, receivership and foreclosure; new Collection Fleet dialog option "hand over the deed".
