# v0.4.0-beta — "The Sector's Economy"

Three systems: the PBC rewards a good relationship, every investment return has a real source in the Sector, and factions finance each other's development.

Branch: `release/v0.4.0-beta` (from `main` at v0.3.2-beta). Effort: S = a day or two, M = a few days, L = a week, XL = more.

**Decisions (user, 2026-10-11):**
- Investment returns come from **real money flows**.
- NPC-to-NPC deals **really build the industry** in the NPC market.
- The PBC relationship raises an **internal underwriting score**; the bureau score stays FICO-pure.
- 0.3.2 (realistic returns) was released first.

**Order:** 0 → A → B → M → C → R. A is small. B is the foundation C needs, because faction treasuries decide who can invest and who can repay.

Rules carried over from v0.3.0:
- Save compatibility guard: only additive, null-safe saved fields.
- NexusUI updated for every visible change.
- No per-frame sector scans: monthly or cached per game day only.
- Strings in en and pt-BR.
- Each section ends with `test.ps1 -Smoke` passing.

## A. Relationship banking (S) ✅
The PBC rewards customers who pay on time and penalizes those who don't. The bonuses are small and grow with the relationship.

**Design**
- [x] Relationship `r` is the PBC's relation to the player, from −1 to +1.
- [x] **Internal score.** The PBC's underwriting score is the bureau score + round(25 × r), clamped to 300–850.
  - It drives eligibility, loan limits, the number of loans, the credit-line limit and the rate bracket.
  - No bureau score (a thin file) means no internal score.
  - The bureau score never includes it. The credit-builder loan's eligibility and the insurance credit factor keep using the bureau score: the builder loan is for thin or damaged files, and insurers price on bureau data.
- [x] **Relationship pricing.** Loan rates × (1 − 0.10 × r).
- [x] **Investment returns.** Expected annual return + 0.5% × r.
- [x] **Behaviour.**
  - +0.002 per bill paid on time (from the second bill on; credit lines only while in use).
  - +0.01 per loan paid off after 3+ months. Taking and repaying at once earns nothing.
  - −0.02 per missed payment.
  - −0.08 at default.
  - Banking alone moves the relation between *Inhospitable* and *Friendly*. Hostility only comes from refusing a Collection Fleet or a foreclosure war. A defaulted borrower in receivership for years ends at −0.49, never hostile.
- [x] The monthly statement shows the relation change since the last month end ("PBC relations +0.4").

**Implementation**
- [x] `CreditScoreManager`: `pbcRelationship()`, `relationshipBonus()`, `getUnderwritingScore()`, `adjustRelation()` (with the cap and floor `RepLevel`s), and `relationChangeThisMonth()`. Two new saved fields, null-safe.
- [x] Underwriting call sites (loan offers, quotes, the branch, credit line, max loans, rate modifier) use the internal score. Displays and logs keep the bureau score.
- [x] `InterestEngine.relationshipPriceFactor()` in loan pricing; the return bonus in `expectedAnnualReturn`.
- [x] Relation changes in `LoanManager` (month-end on-time and late, payoff, default).
- [x] Terminal: an Overview line (relation and level, internal score and bonus, rate effect, return effect); a Credit tab line explaining internal vs bureau score. NexusUI: Internal Score and PBC Relation rows; `pbcRelation`, `pbcRelationLevel`, `internalScore` and `relationshipRateFactor` in the JSON.
- [x] Settings: score bonus, rate discount, return bonus, the event deltas, payoff minimum months, cap and floor levels. The main three are also in LunaLib.
- [x] Found by the tests: the foreclosure auction now restores the relation from before the foreclosure war, which already includes the goodwill lost for the default.

**Tests**
- [x] Bureau score 653: friend (+60) → internal 668, Megacorp at 3.29%/mo, bonds 4.80%/yr; neutral → 3.50%, 4.50%, 3 loans; enemy (−100) → internal 628, 4.62%, 2 loans.
- [x] 12 months paid on time → +0.022. The cap holds at 0.75. Instant take-and-repay → 0. Missed payments + default → −0.14. Two years in default → −0.49 (not hostile).

## B. Real money flows (L) ✅
Every credit an investment earns is paid by someone in the Sector, and every loss goes somewhere. The PBC keeps public books.

**Spikes (BOS-DIAG log from the user's game, 2026-10-11)**
- [x] A temporary `core/EconomyDiagnostics` logs "BOS-DIAG" lines at every month end. **Remove it before the release.**
- [x] `getNetIncome()` is meaningful for NPC markets: 127 NPC markets, 91 positive and 36 negative, about 3.0M a month in total (Chicomoztoc +104k, Kazeron −65k, New Aurora +221k).
- [x] Commodity prices vary by market (fuel 23–42, heavy machinery 68–134). A full Sector index (1,016 samples) takes 1.9 ms: monthly only.
- [x] Iron Shell is active in the user's game (`ironshell`).

**Model (`economy/SectorEconomy`, `FactionTreasury`, `PbcLedger`; one market scan a month)**
- [x] **Faction treasuries.** Each major faction's treasury += the net income of its markets − war costs (3% of gross income per war) − interest on its PBC debt. When short it borrows; when flush, above three months of income, it repays. Above 1.5 years of gross income in debt it defaults: the PBC writes off half, and bondholders lose half. Losing all its markets also means default. Debt stays in `AssetSeizureManager.factionDebts`, so old saves keep it. The random simulation is gone.
- [x] **Bond yield** = 4.5% + 2% per year of gross income owed + 0.5% per war + 1% if unstable + 5% for a year after a default (1–30%).
- [x] **Where each product's return comes from:**

  | Product | Source |
  |---|---|
  | Savings | The deposit rate (policy 3% + half the sovereign spread), paid by the PBC from its interest income. |
  | Bonds | A coupon locked in at purchase, from the issuer's treasury (the fund: every treasury, by economic weight); plus a price change of −5 × the yield change; a 50% haircut on default. |
  | Commodities | The price index change, plus interest on the collateral. |
  | Venture | Earnings yield × (portfolio income / its 12-month average), plus growth; markets lost are written off; 1%/yr fee. The portfolio holds up to 20 NPC markets of size 3–5. |
  | Military | Earnings yield × (military spending / its average), plus growth; 1%/yr fee. Military spending is 5% of gross income, plus the war costs. |

- [x] The relationship bonus is paid by the PBC as a rebate. Every return is recorded in the ledger against its payer.
- [x] **Migration:** debts from the old random simulation in older saves are restructured quietly on the first real month, to at most half a year of gross income.

**Implementation**
- [x] `InterestEngine.calculateInvestmentReturn` delegates to `SectorEconomy.investmentReturn`; `expectedAnnualReturn(type, issuer)` reads the economy.
- [x] `BankAccount`: `bondFactionId`, `bondYield`, `lastReturn`, `lastSourceKey`, `lastSourceArg`. `InvestmentManager.invest(type, amount, issuer)`.
- [x] Player loan interest is recorded in the PBC ledger while billing.
- [x] Month end: `beginMonth`, then `monthlyUpdate` (replacing the random sovereign simulation), then the investments, then `endMonth`.
- [x] Terminal:
  - Overview: the **Sovereign Desk** table (faction, income, treasury, debt to the PBC, yield, wars) and the PBC's books.
  - Investments: each holding shows last month's return and who paid it; bonds show their issuer and coupon; available products show the expected return now, a risk word and who pays; individual factions' bonds can be bought.
  - Quotes show the issuer and the source.
- [x] Branch office: a bond issuer step (the fund or one faction) before the amount.
- [x] NexusUI: investments show last month's return; `investmentFlows` and `economy` (PBC reserves, commodity index, military spending, treasuries) in the JSON; PBC Reserves and Commodity Index rows in the Sector card.
- [x] Settings for every parameter in `bos_settings.json`. The 0.3.2 `investmentWarBonus` was removed: wars now act through real military spending.
- [x] Save baseline: +54 entries, none removed.

## C. Development financing contracts (XL) ✅
Factions finance each other's buildings. The player can receive offers, make offers, and watch NPC factions do it among themselves.

**Participants:**
- All vanilla political factions.
- The modded factions when present: **Aurora** (`uaf`), **Xhan Empire** (`xhanempire`), **Iron Shell** (`ironshell`), **ScalarTech** (`scalartech`). Xhan Empire left the user's list on 2026-10-15; the list there also has Interstellar Imperium, Diable Avionics, MVS, Valkyrian and Arkgneisis with real territory. Section M's `PoliticalFactions` already decides who is a state, so C can take every political faction instead of a fixed list.
- The PBC.
- The player.

Pirates, the Luddic Path and non-political factions (section M: pirate behaviour, Nexerelin's pirate / hostile-to-all flags) never take part.

**Contract lifecycle**

```
OFFERED -> ACCEPTED (funds paid to the recipient) -> BUILDING
   BUILDING, built by the deadline         -> BUILT -> repaid at minimal interest  -> CLOSED (relations up)
   BUILDING, deadline missed               -> DEMAND: relations down, repay with penalty interest by a new deadline
        DEMAND paid                        -> CLOSED
        DEMAND unpaid                      -> COLLECTION (full force): PBC Collector Fleet, credits, forfeited assets
   BUILT, repayment missed                 -> COLLECTION (minimal force): smaller fleet, milder demands
```

**Design** (built 2026-10-15; the user asked "make section c")
- [x] **Terms.**
  - Principal = the building's cost × 1.15 (`developmentContingency`).
  - Construction deadline = build time + 60 days (`developmentSlackDays`), counted from signing.
  - Repayment over 24 months at 1% a month (`developmentRate`, `developmentRepayMonths`); the player as investor picks 0.5, 1 or 1.5%.
  - Missed deadline: 3% a month (`developmentPenaltyRate`) and 60 days to repay in full (`developmentDemandDays`).
  - The PBC acts as escrow and enforcement agent: the Clearing Hall settles inter-faction trade (LORE.md).
- [x] **Offers to the player.** Each month, with a 35% chance (`developmentOfferChance`), the richest Favorable investor with money to lend offers the best project it can afford; at most 2 open contracts with the player as recipient, none while banking is restricted or in bankruptcy. "Best" is the highest expected monthly gain (cost back over 36 months at a size-5 market, scaled by size); the game's own `isAvailableToBuild` covers resource conditions. Lendable = treasury − 3 months of income (the PBC: a tenth of its reserves).
- [x] **Offers to the player** (as first designed): Investors are factions with a treasury surplus (section B) and a relation to the player of *Favorable* or better. They pick one of the player's colonies and a buildable industry with a good expected return, from market size, resource conditions and demand. Offers arrive as intel with Accept and Decline, and list the building, colony, amount, deadlines, rates and consequences. Accepting pays the credits to the player.
- [x] **Player as recipient.**
  - Construction is checked daily: the industry must exist and be functional in that colony by the deadline. Losing the colony is a missed deadline.
  - Repayment reuses the loan machinery: a new loan type `DEVELOPMENT` (not sold at the counter, outside the loan limit), with no installments during construction (nor for 15 days after), then a schedule at the low rate. Every payment goes to the investor's treasury; the interest isn't the PBC's income.
  - Collection reuses the phases and the Collection Fleet. Full force: an unpaid demand puts the whole balance in arrears at the default threshold. Minimal force (`BankAccount.minimalForce`, set when built): half the fleet (`developmentMinimalForce`), half the reputation loss for refusing it.
  - Relations with the investor: +0.03 built, +0.05 repaid, −0.08 deadline missed, −0.12 collection.
- [x] **Player as investor** (as first designed): A "Development Finance" section in the terminal and the branch office. Pick a faction (*Favorable* or better), one of its markets and an industry, then an amount. The NPC accepts or declines based on expected return, cost, its treasury, the offer's terms, the player's strength and the relationship. The NPC then really builds the industry and repays the player from its treasury. If it fails, the PBC recovers from that faction's treasury on the player's behalf, partially if the treasury is short, and relations drop.
  - Built: the terminal's Development tab lists the 8 best projects at size 4+ markets of Favorable factions (cached a day), with the three rates; the branch office has the same flow. Amounts are fixed at the project's principal. The recipient's installments reach the player through the monthly report ("Development contracts"); a recovery arrives at once.
- [x] **NPC to NPC.** Every month, a few deals are proposed across the Sector, capped at a handful active at once, between factions at *Favorable* or better. The recipient builds the industry for real; outcomes drive relations and treasuries; they show up in an intel log and as venture-fund stakes (section B).
  - Built: 1 deal proposed a month (`developmentNpcDealsPerMonth`), at most 4 under way (`developmentMaxNpcActive`). The investor pays the principal, the recipient pays the build cost out of it and the building starts for real; repayments come out of the recipient's treasury (it borrows from the PBC if short); built projects at size 3-5 markets join the venture portfolio. A missed deadline: penalty interest, then the Clearing Hall takes what the recipient's treasury holds. The register is one intel item ("Clearing Hall: development deals").
- [x] **Industries:** farming, aquaculture, mining, refining, light and heavy industry, fuel production, commerce, patrol HQ, waystation, ground defenses (`developmentIndustries`; orbital works is an upgrade, not a build). Skipped when the market has it, can't build it (`isAvailableToBuild`), has no industry slot left (`Misc.getNumIndustries` counts the construction queue), or a contract already targets it.
- [x] **NPC acceptance:** utility = monthly gain × 60 months − build cost − interest (principal × rate × (months + 1) / 2) − 10% of the principal for deadline risk + 10% × relation + 5% × the investor's strength (the player: fleet points / 300; a faction: markets / 10). Accept above 0. With the defaults a size-6 market takes 1% easily, a size-4 one barely takes 1.5%, a size-3 one never.

**Spikes**
- [x] `BaseIndustry.advance` progresses construction on any market (no player check), so `addIndustry` + `startBuilding()` builds for real in the spec's build time. No timer of our own.
- [x] Industries belong to the market: whoever captures it gets the building. The contract follows the recipient faction, so a market lost before completion counts as a missed deadline; after completion repayments continue from the recipient's treasury.
