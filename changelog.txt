Bank of Starsector

0.4.0-beta (in development)
- New: real money flows. Every investment return now has a payer in the Sector: savings are paid by the PBC
  out of its loan interest; government bonds by the issuing faction's treasury (buy one faction's bonds or the
  fund; the coupon is locked in at purchase and the price moves with the faction's yield); commodity futures
  follow a real price index of the Sector's commodities; venture stakes earn what growing NPC markets earn
  (lost markets are written off); military contracts take a share of the factions' military spending, which
  rises in war.
- New: faction treasuries. Each major faction collects its markets' net income, pays for its wars and the
  interest on its debt, borrows from the PBC when short and defaults when its debt outgrows its economy
  (bondholders lose half). Replaces the old random sovereign debt. The Overview shows the Sovereign Desk:
  every faction's income, treasury, debt, bond yield and wars, and the PBC's own books.
- Changed: each investment shows what it earned last month and who paid it (also in NexusUI).
- New: relationship banking. The PBC prices and approves loans on an internal score = credit bureau score +
  up to 25 points for the relationship (minus for a bad one); the bureau score itself is unchanged. A good
  relation also lowers loan rates (up to 10%) and raises investment returns (up to +0.5%/yr); a bad one does
  the opposite. Paying on time improves the relation (+0.2 per payment, +1 per loan carried 3+ months and
  paid off), missed payments (-2) and defaults (-8) hurt it. Banking alone moves it between Inhospitable
  and Friendly. Shown in the Overview, the Credit tab, the monthly statement and NexusUI.
- Compatibility: factions that hold markets without being states are no longer major factions: factions with
  pirate behaviour in their faction file, and those Nexerelin flags as pirates or hostile to everyone (the
  Hiver Swarm, the Starlight Cabal, ArmaA's pirates). A swarm at war with everyone used to add one war per
  faction (13 in a new game with a big mod list) to the war surcharge, every treasury's war costs and every
  bond yield; it also issued bonds, ran up sovereign debt and could bid at foreclosure auctions. Older saves close such a faction's sovereign book
  quietly (no default). New settings politicalFactions / nonPoliticalFactions override the classification.
  On a new game the PBC is hostile to modded raider factions, like the vanilla pirates.
- Compatibility: the war surcharge scales with the size of the Sector. Up to six states (vanilla) each war
  still adds 5%; with more, wars count by their share of all the pairs of states. A new Nexerelin game with a
  big faction list (13 states, 15 wars) gets about 14% instead of the 25% cap. New setting
  warSurchargeReferenceStates (JSON and LunaLib). The terminal, NexusUI and bos_status show the wars and states.
- Compatibility: fleet insurance has an "other insurance" clause. A ship also insured by Nexerelin's insurer
  is covered for the excess only: the premium is charged on what the PBC adds over that policy, and a loss
  pays what the other insurer does not. Shown in the terminal, the branch office and NexusUI.
- Compatibility: a hull the PBC paid for that comes back to the fleet (Ship Mastery System's graveyard, or any
  mod that returns lost ships) has its payout billed back at the next month end.
- New: development financing. Factions finance each other's buildings, and the Confederation's Clearing Hall
  holds the money in escrow and enforces the terms. A faction with a surplus that is Favorable to you may offer
  to pay for a building at one of your colonies: accept, build it before the deadline, then repay at 1% a month
  over 24 months (nothing due while it goes up). A missed deadline makes the whole balance due at 3% a month
  within 60 days; an unpaid demand is collected by full force, a late repayment after it is built by a smaller
  fleet. You can finance projects at Favorable factions' markets at 0.5, 1 or 1.5% a month; they accept when
  it's worth it to them, really build, and repay you monthly, and the Clearing Hall recovers what it can if they
  miss the deadline. NPC factions sign deals among themselves (one a month, four at most under way) and the
  buildings really go up. Relations follow every contract. Terminal Development tab, branch office "Development
  finance", offer and register intel, a NexusUI card and JSON, and 20 development* settings.
- New: Console Commands bos_status (your accounts) and bos_factions (how the Sovereign Desk treats every
  faction that holds markets, and why).
- Fix: uses Nexerelin 0.12.2's non-deprecated corvus-mode check. build.bat now runs build.ps1, which finds the
  optional mods' jars in versioned folders and refuses to install next to another enabled copy of the mod.

0.3.2-beta
- Changed: investment returns follow real long-run asset classes instead of 1-8% a month. Expected returns
  per year: savings 3% (no risk), government bonds 4.5% (7.9% volatility), commodity futures 4% (18%),
  venture fund 12% (35%), military contracts 9% (18%). Sources: Damodaran's 1928-2025 US returns (T-bills,
  10-year Treasuries, S&P 500, small caps), commodity index and venture capital studies.
- Changed: sector effects are realistic too: military contracts up to +5%/yr at the war surcharge cap,
  bonds up to +2%/yr when sovereign debt is high, commodity futures gain from shortages.
- Changed: monthly results are drawn from a normal distribution around the expected return (savings never
  lose money); the terminal, the branch office and the quotes show returns per year.
- New: investmentReturnMultiplier setting (JSON and LunaLib) for players who want richer returns.

0.3.1-beta
- Performance: sector-wide numbers (war surcharge, market disruption) are computed once per game day instead of
  on every rate quote and dashboard refresh; with large mod lists they scanned every market several times a second.
- Performance: the NexusUI dashboard refreshes twice a second only while it is open (every 10 s otherwise).
- Performance: fewer Nexerelin calls when listing major factions; insurance quotes scan the fleet once.

0.3.0-beta
- New: credit-builder loan. For files with no score or a score under 500 (also during bankruptcy recovery):
  10k-50k held at the bank while you pay 12 installments at a fixed 1%/month, released when paid off.
  Builds a credit file; doesn't count toward the loan limit; on default the bank keeps the deposit.
- New: revolving Confederation Credit Line (score 500+; limit 50k/200k/500k by bracket). Monthly statement,
  minimum payment (interest + 1% of the balance, at least 1k), no interest when the statement is paid in full,
  autopay of the full statement or the minimum, +20% limit after 6 on-time statements, limit cut and no draws
  after a late payment. The credit score now weighs credit-line utilization (statement balance / limit) and
  rewards having both installment and revolving credit. Shown in the terminal and in NexusUI.
- New: before any loan, the terminal asks for confirmation and shows the first installment, total interest,
  total repaid, rate and term; before a locked investment, the lock and the early-withdrawal penalty.
- New: the branch office takes loan applications and investments for any amount (slider), with the same
  terms shown before signing.
- New: "Repayment schedule" on every loan (payment, interest, principal and balance per month, plus what is
  still to pay); the Credit tab charts the last 12 monthly reports and names the factor behind each change.
  NexusUI shows the score trend, the last change and months left per loan.
- New: fleet insurance by House Varenne. Standard (60% cover, 10k deductible per ship) or Comprehensive (80%, 5k).
  Premium = rate x cover x fleet base value x credit factor x claims factor x war surcharge, billed at month
  end. Ships lost in battle are paid after recovery; 30-day waiting period for new policies and new ships;
  monthly payout cap; an unpaid premium suspends cover, two cancel it. Terminal Insurance tab, branch office,
  NexusUI card.
- New: colony-secured loans. Pledge a colony (size 4+) for up to 60% of its appraisal (development, structures,
  resources and traits, income, hazard) at a 25% lower rate; the colony gets a PBC Lien. On default: receivership
  (all its income to the bank, -3 stability). With Nexerelin, 60 days later: foreclosure (the PBC turns hostile
  and invades), then the colony is auctioned to a major faction; the price pays the loan, the surplus is
  returned, relations are restored. Abandoning the colony accelerates the loan; losing it to another faction
  leaves the loan unsecured. Terminal, branch office (slider) and NexusUI.
- Changed: "Sovereign Credit Line" renamed "Sovereign Loan" (it is an installment loan).
- Fixed: defaults are reported to the credit bureau when they happen, and a charged-off loan no longer adds
  a new late mark every month.
- Internal: save compatibility guard (tests/SaveCompatCheck) and docs/SAVE_COMPAT.md.

0.2.0-beta
- New: credit score works like a real credit bureau (FICO-style, 300-850). It is computed once a month from a
  credit report: payment history 35%, amounts owed 30%, length of history 15%, new credit 10%, credit mix 10%.
  Taking loans and repaying them instantly no longer farms points: every application is a hard inquiry and adds a
  young account. Lateness is reported from 30 days and fades over 7 years; charge-offs, repossessions and
  bankruptcy stay on file. No score until an account is 6 months old. The terminal shows factors, reasons and tips.
- New: Brazilian Portuguese translation (language setting, or auto from the system locale).
- Fixed: crash ("Conversion = 'r'") when opening bank intel or tooltips with % in the text.
- Fixed: Nexerelin and NexusUI integrations never worked (Starsector blocks reflection in mod scripts); both use direct calls now.
- New: monthly loan installments in the vanilla income report, autopay toggle, one-month grace period.
- New: debt collection in four phases - reminder, banking restriction with penalty interest, Collection Fleet, default.
- New: Collection Fleets hail you (pay, settle, surrender a ship, stall, refuse); destroyed fleets return stronger.
- New: default seizes your investments and garnishes colony income; loans closed this way are marked SEIZED.
- New: Confederation branch office at PBC markets.
- New: sovereign debt market for NPC factions, tied to government bond yields and sovereign defaults.
- New: in-game settings through LunaLib (optional), data/config/bos_settings.json otherwise.
- New: NexusUI page shows amounts due and autopay, with pay/toggle buttons; data also exposed to the NexusUI API.
- New: LunaLib version checker support.
- Fixed: account ids colliding after load, notices piling up, permanent rate doubling, missing intel icon,
  duplicate PBC homeworld in Nexerelin random sector, collection stuck when no PBC market exists.
- Changed: LazyLib is no longer required.
